Signature Claypot Rice

Our crown jewel: an homage to Po Po’s foundational recipe, elevated with a subtle Japanese nuance.

Premium Thai Hom Mali rice is slow-simmered in Banko-yaki earthenware alongside tender boneless chicken thighs, braised shiitake mushrooms, Japanese ebi, rich liver sausage, and XO pork sausages. The result is an exquisite harmony of smoky aromatics, rich umami, and a golden, delicate bottom crust.
